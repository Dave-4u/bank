A practice bank, not a real one.

Kolo is the browser version of my Python bank.py console app. Sign up, log in, deposit, withdraw, and send pretend naira to other accounts on this device.

Passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2 (WebCrypto), just like the Python version. Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage.
